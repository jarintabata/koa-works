Common questions

Things early operators ask.

Does Koa replace AppFolio? +

No. AppFolio remains your system of record. Koa reads from it and writes work back to places you already use — your email drafts folder, your task lists, your phone. Your AppFolio workflows don't change.

What does Koa send on my behalf? +

Nothing, unless you ask. Every email sits as a draft for you to review. Every dispatch to a vendor is queued for your approval. We're an advisor, not an autopilot.

How does it learn my voice? +

On connect, Koa reads 12 months of your sent mail to model how you write to different kinds of counterparts — tenants, vendors, brokers, lenders. Drafts adapt to the recipient.

What data leaves my control? +

All data is tenant-isolated. We don't train shared models on your portfolio. Your emails, leases, and financials are not used to improve anyone else's Koa.

Why AppFolio only? +

For beta, we're going deep on one system. Yardi, MRI, and Buildium support are on the roadmap for H2.

What does Koa cost? +

Beta is free for design partners, with a lifetime discount locked in at launch. Pricing will be announced when we exit beta. Design partners always pay less — that's the deal.
